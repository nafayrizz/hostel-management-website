Hostel Rules & Regulations

All residents are expected to read, understand, and follow these rules. Adherence to these regulations ensures a peaceful and productive environment for everyone.

Last Updated: June 2026
📋

General Conduct

  • 01. All residents must carry their UET ID card and hostel ID at all times on campus.
  • 02. Residents must maintain respectful behavior towards warden, staff, and fellow residents at all times.
  • 03. Loud music, gatherings, or any activity disturbing others is strictly prohibited after 10:00 PM.
  • 04. Smoking, alcohol, and any form of drug use is completely banned on the premises.
  • 05. Any damage to hostel property will be charged to the responsible resident.
🕐

Timings & Gate Policy

  • 01. Main gate closes at 11:00 PM. Residents must return before closing time. Late entry requires prior permission from the warden.
  • 02. Mess breakfast is served from 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM. Dinner is from 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM. No meals will be served outside these hours.
  • 03. Overnight stays outside the hostel require written notice to the warden's office at least 12 hours in advance.
  • 04. External visitors are only permitted in the common area. No visitors are allowed inside rooms or on the upper floors.
  • 05. Visitor hours are 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM only on weekdays. Weekend visitors must have warden's approval.
💳

Fee & Payment Policy

  • 01. Monthly room rent of PKR 8,000 must be paid by the 10th of every month at the warden's office.
  • 02. A late payment fine of PKR 200 per day will be charged after the 10th.
  • 03. Mess charges are calculated daily: PKR 75 per meal (Breakfast or Dinner). Both meals = PKR 150/day.
  • 04. Mess fee must be cleared by the last day of every month. Unpaid mess dues will block clearance approval.
  • 05. A security deposit of PKR 5,000 is required upon check-in. It is refundable at checkout (minus any deductions).
🧹

Cleanliness & Maintenance

  • 01. Each resident is responsible for keeping their own room clean. Rooms will be inspected weekly by the warden.
  • 02. Littering in corridors, stairways, or common areas is strictly prohibited and will result in a fine.
  • 03. Cooking inside rooms is not allowed. Use of personal heating appliances (electric rods, coils) is banned.
  • 04. Plumbing issues, broken fixtures, or electrical faults must be reported to the warden immediately.
  • 05. Washrooms must be kept clean after each use. Common area cleaning is a shared responsibility.

By residing in Zubair Hall, all residents are considered to have read and agreed to these rules. Violation of any rule may result in a fine, suspension, or removal from the hostel at the warden's discretion. For any queries regarding these rules, contact the warden's office.
