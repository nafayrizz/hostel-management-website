About the Hostel

Zubair Hall,
UET Lahore

Zubair Hall is one of the oldest and most respected student hostels at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore. Located within the main campus on Grand Trunk Road, the hostel provides a safe and comfortable living environment for students pursuing engineering and technology degrees.

The hostel features well-maintained rooms, a fully operational mess serving two balanced meals daily, high-speed Wi-Fi, and round-the-clock administrative support. Our goal is to let students focus on academics without worrying about their daily needs.
